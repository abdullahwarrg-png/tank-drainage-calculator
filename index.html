Derivation

Adhdt=−Cda2gh(1)

For an initial head h(0) = h₀, integration gives

h(t)=h0−CdaAg2t(2)
T=ACda2h0g(3)

T is the time for the free surface to reach the orifice centreline, Cd is the discharge coefficient and g is gravitational acceleration. Assumes A and Cd stay constant throughout drainage.

  • Vented tank with constant horizontal area; no inflow or back pressure.
  • Small outlet relative to tank area; pipe friction and changing discharge coefficient are excluded.
  • Head and remaining volume are measured above the outlet centreline. Water below it is excluded.
